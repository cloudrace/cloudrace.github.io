## Social Media Pulse

📡 **Real-Time Developer & Social Intelligence at Scale**

Social Media Pulse is an intelligent signal classification and market intelligence platform. It ingests, analyzes, and categorizes unstructured social signals across Twitter/X, Reddit, and Hacker News in real time. 

Built with an ADK multi-agent orchestrator and powered by Gemini LLMs, it transforms noisy social feeds into an interactive topic constellation canvas—providing instant clarity on AI governance, enterprise adoption, developer sentiment, and security trends.

### The Tech Stack:
- **LLM & Reasoning**: Google Gemini (structured signal classification & extraction)
- **Agent Orchestrator**: Google ADK (Agent Development Kit) multi-agent workflow
- **Visual Analytics**: Interactive topic & cluster constellation graph canvas
- **Deployment**: Google Cloud Run with GCS-backed persistence and Firebase authentication
