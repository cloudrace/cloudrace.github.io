## AI For Everyone Series 🚀

A comprehensive educational resource and video series designed to demystify generative AI and empower builders at all levels. Rather than just theory, each module dives deep into **Why**, **How**, and actionable **Code & Prompts** across the Google Cloud AI ecosystem.

### Key Pathways Covered:
- **Building Agents**: From consumer personal agents with Google Gems, to automated Workspace Workflows, to enterprise-grade ADK multi-agent systems with governance & guardrails.
- **Building Applications**: Fast prototypes with Gemini Consumer Canvas, medium prototypes with AI Studio, AppSheet integrations, and production microservices on Cloud Run.
- **Weekly Hands-On Prompts & Demos**: Step-by-step guides for financial advisors, creative agents, and NL2SQL data assistants.
